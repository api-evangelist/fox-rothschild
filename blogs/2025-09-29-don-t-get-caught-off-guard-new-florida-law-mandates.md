Effective July 1, 2025, Florida is implementing a significant change to its healthcare licensure framework. A new law, enacted through Florida’s House Bill 975, dramatically expands the scope of required background screening for licensed healthcare practitioners. This is a major update that will affect a broad range of healthcare professionals and the organizations that employ them.
