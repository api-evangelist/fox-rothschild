Today, Minnesota’s Office of Cannabis Management announced that Minnesota’s final set of adult-use cannabis rules have been approved, will be publish in the State Register, and will go into effect later this month. After the rules are published and go into effect, the OCM will be in a position to begin issuing licenses to applicants in uncapped license categories.
