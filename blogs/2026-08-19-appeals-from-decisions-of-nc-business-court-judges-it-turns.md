While the best bet on a prediction market like Kalshi might be that trial results are just unpredictable, one thing that seems relatively certain about practice before the Business Court is the appeal path that follows. By statute the Court’s final judgments, and the appealable interlocutory orders of the Court’s judges, head to the North Carolina Supreme Court as of right. But maybe not always , it turns out.
