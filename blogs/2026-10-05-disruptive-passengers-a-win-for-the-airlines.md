A recent decision by the United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit provides important protection for airlines and their employees who report potentially suspicious passenger behavior to law enforcement. Airline employees routinely make judgment calls about passenger behavior. But when does a reasonable security concern become legally protected conduct—and how far does that protection extend if a passenger is injured during the resulting law-enforcement intervention?
