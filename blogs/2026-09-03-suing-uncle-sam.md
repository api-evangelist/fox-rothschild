In the second of our weekly posts on topics from our 2026 Aviation Roundtable, we present the materials from our panel on Suing the United States for FAA Negligence. When negligence by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) contributes to an aviation accident, the Federal Tort Claims Act (FTCA), 28 U.S.C. § 1346(b), may provide a legal avenue for bringing a claim against the United States.
