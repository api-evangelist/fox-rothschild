Companies with strong brands, like franchise companies, are often seen as excellent investment vehicles. Because these brands have recurring revenue and can be “asset-light,” the revenue stream is considered an annuity. Their value is assessed as a multiple of the revenue stream, adjusted and normalized for usual operations (Adjusted EBITDA).
