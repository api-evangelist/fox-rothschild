A Complimentary Webinar Thursday, July 10, 2025 | 1 – 2 pm ET | 10 – 11 am PT It has been one year since Congress passed the FAA Reauthorization Act. Weighing in at over 1,000 pages, the Act contained countless new initiatives and requirements that touch virtually all aspects of the aviation industry. At the same time, we have a new president, the FAA is once again without a Senate confirmed Administrator, there have been several serious accidents, and the Air Traffic Control system is showing its age.
