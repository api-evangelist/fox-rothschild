Artificial intelligence is rapidly changing how employers make workforce decisions. AI tools that screen applicants, evaluate performance, and analyze compensation data are now commonplace. And while these technologies offer real advantages in efficiency and consistency, they also introduce new legal risks—particularly in the class action space.
