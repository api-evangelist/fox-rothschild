California likes to promote employee mobility and last year took steps to prevent “ stay or pay ” provisions in the employment context. Accordingly, AB 692 prohibited certain contracts that required a worker to repay an employer when the employment relationship or work ended. As originally enacted, the law applied to contracts entered into on or after January 1, 2026.
