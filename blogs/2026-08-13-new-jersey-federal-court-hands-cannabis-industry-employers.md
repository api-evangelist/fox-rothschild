The District of New Jersey issued a comprehensive opinion on May 27, 2026, and entered final judgment on August 10, 2026, in Curaleaf Holdings, Inc. v. New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission, declaring New Jersey’s Labor Peace Agreement (LPA) requirement preempted by the NLRA.
