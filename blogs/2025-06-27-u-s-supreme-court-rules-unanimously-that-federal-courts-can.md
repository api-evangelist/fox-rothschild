In 2019, Congress enacted the Promoting Security and Justice for Victims of Terrorism Act (PSJVTA), which created jurisdiction over the Palestinian Authority (PA) and Palestine Liberation Organization (PLO) for terrorism-related lawsuits in U.S. courts, even when the act of terrorism occurred outside the United States. In the first case brought under PSJVTA, the widow of Israeli-American Ari Fuld, who was stabbed to death by a Palestinian teenager in 2018, brought a damages action against the PA and PLO in federal court in New York.
