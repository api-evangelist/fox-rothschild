U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) has officially announced the FY2026 H-1B Cap Registration dates. Below is a summary of some information that you should know: This year’s initial registration period for the H-1B cap lottery will be from noon Eastern Time on March 7 to noon Eastern Time on March 24, 2025 .
