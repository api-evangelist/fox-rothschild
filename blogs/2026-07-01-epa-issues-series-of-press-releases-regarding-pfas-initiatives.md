The EPA outlined its comprehensive PFAS strategy and announced grant funding for over 40 states and territories. The agency plans to advance technology-based effluent limitations for industrial PFAS dischargers, reaffirmed protections for "passive receivers" of contamination, and committed to updating destruction and disposal guidance annually instead of every three years.
