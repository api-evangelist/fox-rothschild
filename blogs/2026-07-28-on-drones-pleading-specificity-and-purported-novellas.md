Drone technology and its wide applications have had a sweeping impact in military and civilian settings. They have helped even the odds for Ukraine in defending against Russia’s invasion, and become enough of a war narrative that it was big news recently when Ukraine unleashed long-range missiles on Russian manufacturers producing key drone components. Effects on the speed and reach of consumer deliveries seem not far behind.
