Companies in the fashion industry face a gauntlet of varying state laws restricting PFAS in apparel, footwear and textiles. These laws differ from state to state in several important dimensions, including scope of products covered, the type of prohibition (outright ban vs. disclosure and/or labeling), threshold levels, exemptions, phasing timelines, and compliance mechanisms.
