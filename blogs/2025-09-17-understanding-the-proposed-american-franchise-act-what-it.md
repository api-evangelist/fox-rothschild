By Sarah Rozek The U.S. franchise sector is a cornerstone of the American economy, supporting over 800,000 small businesses, millions of jobs, and generating approximately $896 billion in annual economic output. However, the industry has faced significant uncertainty in recent years due to shifting federal labor regulations—particularly around the “joint employer” standard.
