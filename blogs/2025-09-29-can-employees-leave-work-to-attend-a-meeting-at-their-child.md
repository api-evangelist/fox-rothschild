Children across the country have begun another school year, which often comes with mandatory meetings and conferences for parents, requiring attendance during their work hours. In many states, employers cannot deny an employee leave to attend such activities. Employers should be aware that laws in several states protect a parent or guardian’s leave for attendance at school conferences.
