If you own a business or reside in Lancaster County, pay close attention: the IRS confirmed in a July 21, 2026 letter that 20 revenue officers will be conducting face-to-face visits with taxpayers in the community during the weeks of August 10 and August 17, 2026 . Recent reporting confirmed the IRS sent similar letters to police departments in Millersville Borough, East Cocalico Township, and Lititz Borough. The Millersville Borough Police Department indicated that the IRS visits are part of a regional effort in which the IRS will be visiting “many municipalities” in the area.
