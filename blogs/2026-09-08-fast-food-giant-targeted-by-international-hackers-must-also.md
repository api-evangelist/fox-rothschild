Bojangles , a Charlotte-born chicken, biscuits, and tea restaurant chain, long ago outkicked its Southeastern roots into nearly 800 locations across 17 states. That footprint, featuring more than 9,000 employees, makes it a game-day favorite for tailgaters and watch parties. But at that business scale, “It’s Bo Time!” can be a clarion call for more than hungry football fans.
