GAO’s bid protest timeliness rules are strict. Even a strong protest can be dismissed if it is not filed within the deadline established by GAO regulations. In certain procurements, required debriefings can affect protest deadlines while voluntary debriefings or brief explanations in other types of procurements do not toll deadlines.
