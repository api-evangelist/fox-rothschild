A new decision from the Central District Court for Central District of California Judge Fred Slaughter in Reisberg v. Renaissance Learning may provide insight into what website and app tracking litigation could look like if California’s SB 690 becomes law and limits certain claims under CIPA, the California Wiretapping Law. The case involved Renaissance, a K-12 educational platform that schools required students to use.
