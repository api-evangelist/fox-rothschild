A recent decision from a German labor court offers an interesting perspective on the use of personal information collected from publicly available social media profiles. While the case arose in the context of employment litigation, the court’s reasoning has broader implications for organizations that collect and use publicly available information, including U.S.-based data brokers. The dispute arose after an individual pursuing a discrimination claim submitted an application for legal aid.
