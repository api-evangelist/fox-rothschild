The cannabis industry continues to face a fundamental challenge: access to the regulated banking system. Despite legalization (adult use and/or medical) in dozens of states, cannabis remains federally illegal, leaving state-licensed businesses largely locked out of traditional financial services. This disconnect has created a cash-heavy environment, increasing risks for businesses, employees, and consumers.
