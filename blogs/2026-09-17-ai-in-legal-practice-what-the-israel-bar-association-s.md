As lawyers increasingly incorporate generative AI into everyday practice, bar associations around the world are considering the same question: how do longstanding professional and ethics rules that have governed the practice of law for hundreds of years, apply when legal work is assisted by AI? A recent memorandum from the Ethics Committee of the Israel Bar Association offers a thoughtful framework. The overarching principle is straightforward: competence, confidentiality, diligence, supervision, and professional judgment remain fully applicable when lawyers use AI.
