Preventable Consequences. The Federal Trade Commission has obtained a landmark $17 million settlement against Xponential Fitness, a franchisor operating popular fitness brands including Club Pilates, Pure Barre, YogaSix, StretchLab, and BFT. This enforcement action underscores the critical importance of compliance with franchise disclosure requirements and serves as a cautionary tale.
