The U.S. Supreme Court has clarified that there is a time limit—specifically, a “reasonable time” under Rule 60(c)(1)—for filing motions to set aside judgments as “void” under Rule 60(b)(4), even where the alleged voidness stems from lack of proper service or personal jurisdiction.
