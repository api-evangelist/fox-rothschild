A court rejected a plaintiff's motion for class certification in a telemarketing dispute, ruling that even unopposed motions require rigorous analysis under the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure. The decision holds that a defendant's silence on consent does not relieve plaintiffs of the burden to show class-wide issues can be resolved with common evidence.
