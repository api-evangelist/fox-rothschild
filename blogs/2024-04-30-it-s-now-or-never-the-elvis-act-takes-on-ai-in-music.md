Effective on July 1, 2024, the Ensuring Likeness Voice and Image Security (ELVIS) Act will amend Tennessee’s Personal Rights Protection Act of 1984 to explicitly include protections for songwriters, performers, and music industry professionals’ voice from misuse of artificial intelligence (AI). It is no secret that the rapid growth of AI is a force to be reckoned with, especially in the creative sector. AI has patently accelerated the music industry towards the future.
