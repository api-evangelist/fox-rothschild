Athletes, entertainers, musicians and other individuals at the vanguard of their fields (whether it be the next Super Bowl MVP quarterback, Grammy-winning artist, or Oscar-winning director), often surround themselves with a team of advisors. That team often includes an attorney, agent and manager. While their roles can sometimes overlap, each brings distinct value.
