In Big Apple Tompkins Realty LLC v. Commissioner , the Tax Court addressed a question of first impression: whether I.R.C. § 6234(a)’s 90-day petition filing deadline for partnerships subject to the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2015 (BBA) audit rules is jurisdictional.
