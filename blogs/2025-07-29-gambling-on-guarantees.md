The current dispute between the Las Vegas Raiders (“Raiders”) and Christian Wilkins (“Wilkins”) is more than a football headline; it underscores how fragile the concept of “guaranteed” money can be—not only in sports but also in entertainment law more broadly. Whether you’re an athlete, artist, or entertainer, guarantees are only as strong as the language in your contract and your compliance with the terms. This legal tension reveals a critical need for athletes to understand precisely how “guarantees” actually function in professional sports contracts.
